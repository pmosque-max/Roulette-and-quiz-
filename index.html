Four Cultural Portraits: Personalities, Music, and Flavors

🇯🇲 1. Andrea from Jamaica

Andrea is from Jamaica and she is deeply resilient and cheerful. Her favorite national dish is traditional Escovitch Fish, which is made of fresh red snapper and comes with a spicy pickled sauce of carrots, onions, and scotch bonnet peppers. She thinks that Escovitch Fish is tastier and crunchier than regular steamed fish. When relaxing at home, Andrea listens to classic reggae, claiming that reggae rhythms are more relaxing than rock music. Jamaica is world-renowned for its musical heritage, and Bob Marley is celebrated as the most talented and influential reggae singer in the history of the island.

🇨🇴 2. Mateo from Colombia

Mateo is from Colombia and he is remarkably creative and hardworking. His favorite meal is the hearty Bandeja Paisa, which contains red beans, white rice, ground beef, fried egg, plantain, and avocado. Mateo explains that a Colombian Bandeja Paisa is heavier than a light sandwich because it has large portions of protein and carbohydrates. He loves listening to traditional cumbia and lively vallenato, arguing that Caribbean cumbia is faster and more cheerful than slow acoustic ballads. In Colombian culture, Gabriel García Márquez is remembered as the most famous author of all time.

🇻🇪 3. Gabriela from Venezuela

Gabriela is from Venezuela and she is very friendly and optimistic. Her favorite traditional food is the Arepa, which consists of crunchy cornmeal dough and is made with savory shredded beef, black beans, and white cheese. In her opinion, hot stuffed arepas are more versatile than simple plain bread because they can take any filling. Gabriela enjoys listening to folk joropo and tropical salsa, mentioning that tropical salsa is more energetic than calm elevator jazz. In Venezuelan history, Simón Bolívar is regarded as the most courageous historical leader in the nation.

🇺🇸 4. Marcus from the United States

Marcus is from the United States and he is extremely dynamic and confident. His favorite classic meal is the Cheeseburger, which has a grilled beef patty, melted cheddar cheese, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, and toasted sesame buns. Marcus notes that a homemade cheeseburger is juicier than plain sliced ham. He spends his afternoons listening to modern hip-hop and vintage jazz, saying that urban hip-hop beats are more dynamic than instrumental orchestral tracks. When talking about historical music legends, Louis Armstrong is considered the most creative trumpet player in American jazz culture.
